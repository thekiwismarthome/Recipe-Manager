# Your recipe library

The home screen shows every recipe as a photo card. On a phone it fills the screen with a bottom bar. On a tablet or desktop (a card at least 620 pixels wide) a sidebar appears with search and navigation, and you can collapse it to a thin strip.

## Search

Type in the search box to narrow the list as you type. It looks at each recipe's name, description and tags, so "pasta" finds the pasta dishes and "quick" finds anything tagged quick. It does not search inside the ingredient list.

## Filter

The chips under the search box filter the library:

- **All** shows everything.
- **Courses**, **Categories** and **Collections** open a row of chips. Pick one, for example *Dinner*, *Italian* or *30 Minutes*.
- **Favourites** and **Recent** show the recipes you have hearted or opened lately.
- On a phone the tags row sits below as well. Tap a tag such as *quick* to filter by it.

### Courses, categories, collections and tags

They are four ways to group a recipe, and you choose what each one means:

| Field | Think of it as | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Course | When it is eaten | Breakfast, Dinner, Dessert |
| Category | What kind of food | Italian, Vegetarian, Baking |
| Collection | A pile you curate | Christmas, Weeknight, 30 Minutes |
| Tag | Anything else | freezer friendly, kids love it |

You set them when you add or edit a recipe. Separate several with commas.

## Filter and sort sheet

In Fresh and Compact the tune button opens a sheet with three groups:

- **Show:** all, favourites, recent, or a course, category or collection.
- **Sort by:** favourites first, highest rated, quickest, or A to Z.
- **Minimum rating:** only show recipes with at least that many stars.

## Favourites and ratings

Tap the heart on a recipe to make it a favourite. Favourites get their own section at the top of the library. You can turn that section off in **Settings → Appearance → Recipe grid**. Ratings run from one to five stars, and you set them on the recipe.

## Grid columns

Change how many recipes fit across the screen (2 to 10) under **Settings → Appearance → Recipe grid**. Two or three works well on a phone and four or more on a wall tablet.

## Tonight

In Fresh, the top of the library shows a **Tonight** card. It names the dinner you planned for today, says how many of this week's seven dinners are planned, and has a **Cook** button that opens the recipe. If nothing is planned it says so and offers a shortcut to the [meal planner](meal-planner).
