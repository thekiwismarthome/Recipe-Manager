# Cooking from a recipe

Tap a recipe to open it. Everything you need while cooking is on this one screen.

## The top of the page

You see the photo, the name, and three buttons:

- **Favourite** hearts the recipe.
- **Plan** puts it on the [meal planner](meal-planner).
- **Edit** opens the editor ([adding and editing recipes](adding-recipes)).

Below them are the description (it understands simple Markdown such as bold text and links), the prep, cook and total times, how many it serves, and the course, category and collection chips. On a wide screen the layout uses two columns, with the photo and details on top and the ingredients beside the method.

## Ingredients

The **Ingredients** tab lists everything with its quantity.

- **Scale the servings.** Press **+** or **−** beside the knife-and-fork icon. Every quantity changes to match, so a recipe for four becomes a recipe for six.
- **Convert to metric.** Cups, ounces, pounds and fluid ounces become grams, millilitres and kilograms. The button appears when you switch it on under **Settings → Advanced → Unit conversion**. Pressing it again goes back.
- **Tick things off.** Tap an ingredient to cross it off as you put it in. Section headings such as "Marinade" and "Sauce" group long lists.
- **Add to shopping list.** The cart button lets you choose ingredients and send them to your [shopping list](shopping).

## Directions

The **Directions** tab shows the method, one step at a time.

::: tip Tap a time to start a timer
Any time written in a step, such as "simmer for 15 minutes", is tappable. Tap it and a timer starts. See [Timers](timers).
:::

## Notes, nutrition and photos

- **Notes** holds your own comments on the recipe.
- **Nutrition** shows the nutrition facts per serving, with a ring that shows the share of carbohydrates, fat and protein. It only appears when the recipe has nutrition information.
- **Photos** shows every picture of the dish. Swipe between them, or let the slideshow advance on its own. Set the speed (3, 5, 10 or 15 seconds, or off) under **Settings → Advanced → Image slideshow**.

## Keep the screen on

Under **Settings → Advanced → Keep screen on**, turn on *Keep screen on while viewing a recipe* so the tablet does not go to sleep mid-recipe. You can choose how many minutes before the screen lock is released.

::: warning Needs HTTPS
Browsers only allow this on a secure connection. If the option says it is not available, open Home Assistant over HTTPS, for example through Nabu Casa or your own certificate. This is the most common reason it does not work.
:::
