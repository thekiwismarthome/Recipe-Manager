# Appearance

Everything here is under **Settings → Appearance**. Open **Settings** from the sidebar or the menu button. Your choices are kept in that browser, so a wall tablet and your phone can each look different.

## Interface style

| Style | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| **Fresh** | Soft cards, rounded photos, a Tonight card, and a floating menu. The default, and light to begin with. |
| **Compact** | A denser list with collapsible recipe rows, checkboxes and a flat tab bar. Dark to begin with. |
| **Classic** | The original look, with its own set of colour themes. |

Each style has a small preview. Tap one to switch. The change is instant.

## Theme

Fresh and Compact share three themes:

- **Meadow**, a fresh green
- **Slate**, a periwinkle blue
- **Sunset**, a warm terracotta

A theme changes the accent colour (buttons, the Tonight card, the active tab, ticks and progress bars). Fresh starts on Meadow and Compact on Slate. The same themes are used by [Shopping List Manager](https://thekiwismarthome.github.io/shopping-list-manager-card/), so the two cards look like one product.

**Classic** has its own list: Soft Pastel and Blossom, which follow light and dark; the light themes Arctic, Meadow and Ocean Blue; and the dark themes Midnight Ocean, Ember and Purple &amp; Cyan.

## Colour mode

- **Light** and **Dark** are fixed.
- **Match device** follows Home Assistant's own theme first, and the device's light or dark setting if Home Assistant gives no answer.

## Text size

**S**, **M** or **L** scales the text through the card. Use L on a kitchen tablet you read from across the room.

## Language and units

The card is translated into English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Dutch and Portuguese. The first time it opens it picks the language Home Assistant uses, and you can change it here.

**Preferred units** sets Imperial or Metric. To get a one-tap conversion button on recipes, turn on **Show metric conversion button** in **Settings → Advanced** (see [Cooking from a recipe](cooking)).

## Recipe grid and navigation

- **Recipe columns** sets how many recipes fit across the screen, from 2 to 10.
- **Favourites section** shows or hides the favourites at the top of the library.
- **Recent recipes count** sets how many recipes the Recent filter keeps.
- **Meal planner** adds or removes the Planner tab.

## Advanced

**Settings → Advanced** holds the timer alarm sound, the [Voice PE timers](timers#voice-pe-timers) switch, keep screen on, the metric conversion button and the photo slideshow speed.
