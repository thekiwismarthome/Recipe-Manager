# Shopping list

Recipe Manager can send a recipe's ingredients to a shopping list. It has a simple list of its own, and it works even better with [Shopping List Manager](https://thekiwismarthome.github.io/shopping-list-manager-card/).

## Adding ingredients

1. Open a recipe. Press the **cart** button next to the servings controls.
2. Tick the ingredients you need. Nothing is ticked at first, so you only add what is missing from the cupboard. **Select All** helps with long lists.
3. Press **Add to shopping list**. A tick confirms it went through.

If you scaled the servings first, the scaled quantities are what go on the list. Section headings are skipped.

## The built-in list

Without anything else installed, ingredients go to **My Shopping List**. Open the **Shopping** tab to see them. Tick items as you pick them up, press **Clear checked** to remove the ticked ones, or **Clear all** to start again. The list is kept in the browser on that device.

## With Shopping List Manager

If [Shopping List Manager](https://thekiwismarthome.github.io/shopping-list-manager-card/) is installed, the **Shopping** tab becomes its full card, inside Recipe Manager, and it follows your chosen look and colour mode. You choose which list the ingredients go into, and those lists are shared across the household and every device.

A few details that make it tidy:

- Each item gets a note saying which recipe it came from, for example *[RM] Creamy Tomato Pasta — 28 oz crushed tomatoes*.
- If the same ingredient is already on the list and not ticked, the note is added to that item and no duplicate is created.
- Before adding, you see a short review where each ingredient can be matched to a product in the Shopping List Manager catalogue, so it lands in the right category.
- Fresh and Compact need a recent Shopping List Manager beta to match the style. Older versions still pick up the colours.
